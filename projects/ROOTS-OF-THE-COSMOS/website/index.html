THE VISION

What is Roots of the Cosmos?

Roots of the Cosmos is an immersive 360° experience that explores the connection between the cosmos, the Earth, and the human experience.

Through sound, image, culture, nature, and memory, the experience invites us to look beyond separation and reconnect with the deeper story we share.

It is a journey through ancient memory and contemporary expression — a space where different perspectives can meet, connect, and become part of something larger.

ONE ORIGIN • ONE HUMANITY
